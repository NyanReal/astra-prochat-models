# Water revision

The creek uses a continuous, subdivided meandering sheet with three eased, uneven drops; the edges and heights match at the reach joins. The animated turquoise shader adds bed texture, directional flow, aeration and elongated noise that merges and breaks the white strands. The uneven lips and varying stream widths remove the uniform pale curtain. A rounded spring boundary is enclosed by seven small headwater rocks. Low submerged rocks support the water rather than forming an exposed wall. The final reach extends inside the lake boundary, fades with fragment alpha and gently dips into the lake surface. The terrain channel is cut after the shore blend and shore rocks are excluded from its center. Terrain boulders are also excluded from the truck footprint.

Lake-facing broken foam strips now sit beyond the shoreline rock footprints. Island foam follows individual boulder faces with varying arc length, width and spacing, rather than an evenly dashed ellipse. Clustered transparent patches and curved wisps mark the cascade landings. Foam is batched as a UV-mapped transparent mesh and subtly moves and changes opacity. Its RGBA CanvasTexture is recorded in `material.userData.ownedTextures`.

The creek shader identifies itself with `material.userData.kind = 'creek'`. It uses `position`, `uv`, and float `aeration` attributes; `vFlow`, `vWorld`, and `vAir` varyings; and `uTime` and `uBed` uniforms. It uses the normal Three projection/model-view expression and tone-mapping/color-space chunks. The original lake shader remains unchanged. Lake, creek, and foam use three water mesh draw calls; supporting rocks use an additional instanced draw call.

Verification: `node --check dist/js/terrain.js` passed. Actual high-quality scene constructors and geometry exporter ran successfully and checked exported geometry and transforms for finite values. The last creek center at segment parameter 1.4 is within the lake (lake metric about 0.95). Close-up rendering verifies visible textured strands, contained headwater, exposed broken foam and an unobstructed mouth. The root agent and an independent source reviewer found no actionable geometry, transparency, animation or texture-disposal defects. Live browser rendering remains unverified because the available browser disables WebGL.
